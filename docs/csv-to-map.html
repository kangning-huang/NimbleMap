How · .csv / .tsv / .xlsx

Drag it open on Mac or Windows

Drag the spreadsheet into NimbleMap. It picks latitude and longitude columns (or you choose them). Points land on the map; colour by a field; stack with other layers.

Why not a browser tab or QGIS

Free, local, and enough for a look

Online “CSV to map” tools are crowded and upload your rows. Google My Maps owns “CSV to Google Maps” — we do not chase that. NimbleMap is for desktop CSV coordinates to map: large local files, offline, and overlay with a Shapefile that browsers handle poorly.

Questions

Common questions

How do I plot CSV latitude longitude on a map?

Drag the CSV into NimbleMap. It detects lat/lng columns and draws the points.

CSV to map without uploading?

Yes. Desktop NimbleMap keeps the file on your computer and works offline.

Can I overlay CSV points on a Shapefile?

Yes. Open both; NimbleMap lines them up when the Shapefile has a .prj.
