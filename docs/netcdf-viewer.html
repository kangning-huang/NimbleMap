How · .nc

Drag it open on Mac or Windows

Drag the .nc into NimbleMap. Use the time slider to step or play through the days. Colour by value; click a cell to read it.

Why not a browser tab or QGIS

Free, local, and enough for a look

Panoply is the science-lab default. Browser NetCDF tools exist for a peek. NimbleMap is for when you want a casual NetCDF map viewer — drag-open, time slider, overlay with other layers — without installing a science stack or uploading the file.

Questions

Common questions

Is there a NetCDF viewer for maps without Panoply?

Yes. NimbleMap opens .nc as a map with a time slider. No Java required.

NetCDF viewer for Mac or Windows?

Both. Drag the file in; it works offline.

Can I try a NetCDF map online first?

Use Try in browser for a quick peek, then download when files should stay on your computer.
