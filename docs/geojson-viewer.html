How · .geojson / .json

Drag it open on Mac or Windows

Drag the .geojson into NimbleMap. Features land on the map with their properties in a table. Stack a Shapefile, GeoTIFF or CSV on top when you need more context.

Why not a browser tab or QGIS

Free, local, and enough for a look

Browser GeoJSON viewers (geojson.io and friends) own the online head. NimbleMap wins when you want a desktop GeoJSON viewer — offline, no upload, and the same file stacked with other formats without leaving the app. QGIS is overkill for a quick look.

Questions

Common questions

Is there a free GeoJSON viewer for Mac or Windows?

Yes. NimbleMap opens .geojson with a drag. Free, local, no account.

GeoJSON viewer online vs desktop?

Use the browser demo for a quick peek. Download the desktop app when files should stay offline or you want to overlay CSV, Shapefile or GeoTIFF.

Do I need QGIS to open GeoJSON?

No. Drag it into NimbleMap — no project setup.
